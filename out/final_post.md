Michael Potter owns the finance case for Bitdeer's fee simple purchase of 200 acres in Milam County.
The September 1st, 2026 decision trades lease risk for land control.

Potter is chief financial officer of Bitdeer Technologies Group. The company is headquartered in Singapore, but this decision lands beside its existing Rockdale facility in Milam County.

Bitdeer completed the cash purchase for about $100 million. The parcel is greenfield property next to the company's existing site, and its filing says the combined location has 563 MW of interconnected capacity, with existing plans that could reach 742 MW. The filing also says the site has a dedicated water supply.

The alternative was to keep relying on leased land and accept renewal risk. Potter's explanation is more revealing than a generic expansion announcement. Fee simple ownership lets Bitdeer plan and finance AI and high performance computing infrastructure while keeping Bitcoin mining on the property during development.

The corporate filing calls the parcel greenfield property. Data Center Dynamics independently reports that it was formerly part of the larger Alcoa aluminum smelting property. The distinction matters because Bitdeer is buying undeveloped acreage within an established industrial setting, not a finished campus.

The execution assessment is mixed. The acquisition resolves site control with cash and puts the expansion beside existing transmission and substation infrastructure. It does not deliver a tenant, construction start, new power allocation or operating AI data center.

That sequencing is both the bet and the risk. Control comes first, while customer demand and buildout evidence must follow.

Milam County now carries a larger concentration of energy intensive compute optionality under one operator. The purchased land can support a clearer financing and development path. It also concentrates unanswered questions about added load, water use, construction and whether AI infrastructure will replace or merely sit beside crypto mining.

The next check is Bitdeer's next SEC or monthly operations filing. Look for a named AI tenant, project financing, a construction milestone, an additional power allocation or a parcel specific schedule. Until then, 200 acres is control over an option, not proof of an AI campus.
