Lee McIntire gave Fermi's first AI tenant more time.
The TensorWave closing extension puts the next test on October 31st.

On September 22nd, 2026, the chief executive of Fermi Inc. announced an agreement to move the closing from September 30th to October 31st, 2026. The lease concerns Project Matador in the Texas Panhandle.

McIntire owns the execution. Fermi's subsidiary is the landlord, and TensorWave's subsidiary is the tenant. The company said both sides agreed to the extension while talks continued with an investment-grade counterparty over a lease guaranty.

The available alternative was to keep the earlier deadline. Fermi's filing says either side could terminate if closing conditions were not met by the deadline. Extending it preserves time to finish the deal, while leaving the other lease terms in place.

That is a defensible choice with an unresolved financing test. The closing conditions include work letters and guaranties, operating schedules, a service-level agreement, board approvals and project funding sufficient to build the leased premises. They must be satisfied or waived for the lease to take effect.

For Texas, the immediate consequence is a longer path to certainty around the campus's first customer. Fermi's filing places the project on land leased from the Texas Tech University System in Carson County. The university can terminate the ground lease if the required notice to proceed is not received by December 31st, 2026.

That notice has its own conditions, including a first tenant of at least 200 megawatts and financing for the first phase. The TensorWave lease covers 222 megawatts of total facility power. Capacity alone does not complete the required steps.

The execution assessment is mixed. Keeping a customer at the table has value, but a revised date supplies neither a signed guaranty nor construction funding. American Buildout's October 3rd reporting still described the lease as unclosed. The sources reviewed do not establish a completed closing.

The next check is a clear disclosure by October 31st. It should identify the guarantor and financing, explain any waived conditions, and state whether the lease closed or needs another extension. Building delivery remains planned for late 2027 into early 2028.
