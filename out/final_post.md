Chad Seely at ERCOT is accountable for the operational sequence that puts verification at the front of the line.

The sequence he described in August decides which Texas data center requests keep their place.

ERCOT answered Governor Abbott's August 3rd, 2026 directive for a comprehensive verification and audit of data centers in the interconnection process. ERCOT issued a Market Notice on August 3rd, 2026 that paused the Batch Zero study process. On August 10th, 2026 it asked the Public Utility Commission for good cause exceptions to PGRR 145, including relief from the August 7th classification deadline.

Classification decides how each large load is treated in Batch Zero. ERCOT sorts every load into one of three groups, which are base load, studied load, and a load that does not qualify. In that August filing, ERCOT asked to conditionally classify large loads by August 31st, 2026, so eligibility checks could finish before final classification. ERCOT's August 20th, 2026 presentation said that was the reason.

Seely is listed by ERCOT as senior vice president of regulatory policy, general counsel, and chief compliance officer. In August he told the Texas Tribune that verification would come before the interconnection study begins. He told Utility Dive that ERCOT would not finish the Batch Zero study by April 9th, 2027, and that its goal was a December 10th, 2026 filing with the Public Utility Commission.

The pre-directive baseline is that verification sat inside ERCOT's existing review queue, not at the top of it, according to the Texas Tribune. The governor's directive moved it to the top.

Under the audit, projects that fail verification are to be denied grid connection, and large loads that do not pass, or do not respond, are disqualified from Batch Zero. The Tribune reported that the audit covers about 250 to 300 projects, most of them data centers, representing about 200 gigawatts of future demand.

This is a public grid process, so the test is accountability. Did the schedule hold, and did the criteria get disclosed? In August ERCOT said it expected to send requests for information in late August and early September, with possible further rounds in October and November. Seely also said in August that ERCOT was still working on a new Batch Zero timeline.

The next check is the Batch Zero Eligibility Verification Report that ERCOT said in August it planned to file by December 10th, 2026. Will the December filing name the disqualification criteria, so a developer can check its own load against them before the April 9th, 2027 study deadline?
