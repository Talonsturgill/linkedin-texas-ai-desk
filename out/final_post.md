Noah Schochet is putting TerraFirma's $100 million Series A behind a builder that makes its own robots.
The test is a finished Texas job.

On July 14th, 2026, his company announced funding to expand engineering, manufacturing, operations and construction teams. Schochet is TerraFirma's CEO and cofounder. The capital backs more semi-autonomous heavy equipment and the people who put it to work.

There is a local base for that plan. Hays Free Press reports that TerraFirma opened its new Buda headquarters on July 10th, 2026. The site brings research, design and testing together, with outdoor space for equipment trials.

The company combines AI software for planning work, a remote control center and retrofitted machines. Its release names recent jobs in North Austin, Spicewood and New Braunfels. Those are company-reported projects, not independent proof of savings.

The real choice is how to sell the work. A robotics firm can supply technology for other contractors to use. Schochet told Inc. that TerraFirma instead builds its own technology to perform construction projects. This funding scales that existing model.

That puts the software team close to the dirt. Running jobs can expose failures that a controlled test misses and feed those lessons into the next version. The same choice also leaves TerraFirma responsible for crews, equipment and deadlines. More capital does not make those duties disappear.

The execution case is promising but unproven at scale. Inc. reports that remote human control remains available for tasks the machines cannot yet automate. The useful question is how often that help is needed across a full job, including the hard parts.

Schochet has set an end-of-October 2026 target for fleets on three continents. That is his stated goal. Deployment would show reach. It would not, on its own, show that a Texas customer paid less or got a safer site.

The next check is whether those fleets enter service, followed by a completed Texas job with cost, schedule and human-intervention records. The sources reviewed do not supply that full comparison. A repeat customer could help test whether the result holds beyond a demonstration.
