Leann Kidd is preparing Northside Independent School District to launch a districtwide managed ChatGPT environment for staff.
The October rollout puts one controlled front door around tools already in use.

Northside made the commitment public on August 27th, 2026. Kidd is the district's Executive Director of Technology Services in San Antonio, where she leads teams that implement education technology.

The immediate scope is staff, not districtwide student access. Northside serves 97,600 students across 133 schools with 13,000 employees. Texas Public Radio and San Antonio Report say more than half of staff had already used AI, including individual ChatGPT accounts, Gemini Notebook and MagicSchool AI.

That existing mix is the real alternative. Northside could have continued with individual accounts, separate approved tools and training alone. It chose a managed ChatGPT environment while keeping Gemini available.

The district describes the new environment as a controlled space with administrative guardrails. Staff will reach it through Northside's application portal. The district says those controls are intended to support its student privacy and data governance responsibilities.

That is a design claim before launch, not a measured result. The fetched records do not publish detailed rules for retention, access logs, incident escalation, training completion or usage review. Central access can make governance easier to see. It does not make every prompt appropriate or every output correct.

Kidd has already named part of the human layer. Staff training covers prompting, bias, privacy, ethics and etiquette. An internal task force discusses AI and recommends policy. Kidd also said any move toward broad student access would be slower, even if the product later offered it.

The next check arrives in October. Does the portal give the right people the right access, keep human review visible and produce records that let Northside test its own safety claims? The district should also face a continuity choice before the free period ends in June 2028.

This is a public implementation decision, not a verdict on whether schools should use AI. The useful evidence will be what Northside publishes about controls, training, incidents, staff use and renewal costs after the system is live.
