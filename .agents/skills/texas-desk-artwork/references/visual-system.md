# Texas Desk visual system

The fixed furniture is small. Recognition comes from the `TEXAS AI DOCKET` wordmark, one solid
five-pointed star, Fraunces display type, JetBrains Mono telemetry, and the Big Bend dusk base
register. The story artwork changes every day.

Use the story's own Texas material world. A Panhandle transmission decision can use wind-cut
plains, pylons, survey marks, and hard horizontal light. A Gulf Coast compute project can use
humid industrial light, cooling water, port geometry, and storm-scale atmosphere. A Hill Country
water decision can use limestone, spring water, contour lines, and fractured strata. A robotics
decision can show the actual application setting named by the source. Do not force every story
into Austin or a desert.

The preferred palette begins at `#08060F`, `#0F0C1C`, `#191530`, and `#2B2447`, with caliche
`#E4D8C3`, limestone `#EDE6D6`, dust `#C9B393`, rust `#8C5A3C`, dusk ember `#B4664F`, dusk gold
`#E0956A`, bluebonnet `#4E5FA8`, and mesquite `#6E7A5A`. Use two to six of them or a restrained
story-derived extension. Texas flag red `#BF0A30` is reserved for a genuinely urgent open
deadline and should almost never appear in a profile cover.

The image must not use a generated likeness of the subject without a supplied, inspectable
reference and an explicit reason to make portraiture the story. The default is a decision
metaphor, mechanism, or real setting. Avoid generic technology imagery and Texas tourism cues.
