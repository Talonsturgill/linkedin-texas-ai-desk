# Texas Desk visual system

Recognition comes from the `TEXAS AI DOCKET` wordmark, one solid five-pointed star, Fraunces display
type, JetBrains Mono telemetry, and the dark or light publication furniture from `compose_cover.py`.
The story art changes every day and must be an object or mechanism from the decision, drawn in code.

Use the story's own material and setting only when the dossier establishes it. Do not draw a located
Texas property, a campus, a map, or a figure the dossier does not verify. Conceptual objects such as
a key, a bell, a calendar tab, or plan sheets are acceptable when they stand for a verified mechanism.

Palette: start from the brand inks `#08060F`, `#0F0C1C`, `#191530`, `#2B2447`, caliche `#E4D8C3`,
limestone `#EDE6D6`, dust `#C9B393`, rust `#8C5A3C`, dusk ember `#B4664F`, dusk gold `#E0956A`,
bluebonnet `#4E5FA8`, mesquite `#6E7A5A`, plus restrained story-derived extensions such as teal or brass.
Texas flag red `#BF0A30` is reserved for a genuinely urgent open deadline and should almost never appear.

No generated likeness of the subject. No boots, hats, longhorns, derricks, state silhouettes, or
tourist imagery. No emblems or symbols that imply endorsement.
